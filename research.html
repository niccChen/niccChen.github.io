Scientific machine learning, trustworthy language models, and uncertainty-aware prediction.

SCIENTIFIC COMPUTING

ATSolver: Agentic Tuning of Sparse Linear Solvers with Numerical Feedback

An LLM-guided autotuner for sparse linear solvers that combines numerical verification, matrix structure, retrieval, and execution memory. Improved solve success from 61.0% to 71.4% across 105 runs under a six-proposal budget.

EPIDEMIC FORECASTING

EPI-SST: Renewal-Structured State-Space Transformers for Epidemic Forecasting

A Transformer–state-space model combining renewal-based dynamics and uncertainty estimation. Evaluated against nine baselines, with CRPS reductions of 8–43% versus the best probabilistic baselines across 12 dataset–horizon settings.

Under review · AAAI AISI 2027Read more ↗
CLINICAL MACHINE LEARNING

Multimodal Dynamic Clinical Prediction

Dynamic patient-state modeling from structured EHR and clinical notes, preserving uncertainty in partially observed event labels. Achieved the highest mean AUPRC on 11 of 13 evaluated clinical endpoints in MIMIC-IV.

A fine-tuning framework using KL-based auditing, gradient-compatible group selection, and constraint projections to reduce subgroup calibration errors.
