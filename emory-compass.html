Emory Compass

Academic & career discovery

A source-grounded discovery and planning application that helps Emory students explore faculty research, academic and career events, and internship resources.

Emory Compass brings selected public Emory resources into a searchable catalog with source excerpts and verification dates. Its Explore, Ask Compass, and My Plan interfaces connect discovery with saved opportunities and next steps.

The agent combines structured turn planning, a persistent FAISS index, learned reranking, requirement-to-passage alignment, and evidence-bounded generation with a separate verification stage.

The application uses a FastAPI backend, Next.js frontend, and Supabase for catalog data and personal plans. Catalog lifecycle checks hide past events and flag resources that need re-verification. The curated catalog demonstrates the workflow rather than claiming complete coverage of Emory opportunities.
