EPI-SST: Renewal-Structured State-Space Transformers for Epidemic Forecasting

First author · Aug 2025 – Mar 2026

A Transformer–state-space model combining renewal-based dynamics and uncertainty estimation. Evaluated against nine baselines, with CRPS reductions of 8–43% versus the best probabilistic baselines across 12 dataset–horizon settings.
